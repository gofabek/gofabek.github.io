Girih

An arrow puzzle in the style of Uzbek girih patterns: 1000 levels and 200 landmarks of Uzbekistan.

Coming soon to Google Play Privacy policy ↗

The Google Play link will appear here once the app is released.

Every arrow slides out in the direction it points — if nothing stands in its way. Find the right order, clear the board, and landmarks like Registan, Kalta Minor and Shah-i-Zinda appear from the arrows. Works offline and runs light even on budget phones.

Problem

Most puzzle games rush you with a timer and need the internet — and Uzbek patterns and landmarks hardly ever appear in them.

→ Solution

Girih works offline with no timer. Every level is drawn in Uzbek girih style, and clearing the board reveals one of Uzbekistan’s landmarks.

Features

  • 1000 levels, from easy to tricky
  • 200 landmarks with photos and stories
  • Daily challenge and 16 achievements
  • Works offline, no timer
