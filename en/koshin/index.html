Koshin

A calm 8×8 block puzzle: place pieces, clear lines. With daily challenges and themes.

Coming soon to Google Play Privacy policy ↗

Place pieces on an 8×8 board — full rows and columns clear. The generator always deals a piece that fits, and combos and full board clears earn bonus points. Sound and music are synthesized in code.
