Shop Bot

A Telegram ordering system for cafés, shops and salons: customers order in the bot, owners manage the menu in a Mini App.

A SaaS for small businesses: every café or shop gets its own Telegram bot. Customers browse the menu, fill a cart and place an order. The owner receives orders in Telegram, updates their status with buttons and manages the menu through a Mini App. One server serves hundreds of businesses, and each business’s data is isolated at the database level. No commission — a monthly subscription.

Features

  • Menu, cart and checkout right inside the bot
  • Owner Mini App: menu, prices and photos
  • One server, hundreds of businesses (multi-tenant)
  • Encrypted tokens and HMAC verification

Tech stack

  • Python
  • aiogram
  • FastAPI
  • PostgreSQL
  • React
  • TypeScript
  • Docker
  • Caddy
